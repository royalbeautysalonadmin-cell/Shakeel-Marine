# Shakeel Marine SEO Audit

Audit date: 2026-08-30

## Technical SEO completed

- Canonical URLs are generated from `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` for every indexed page.
- `robots.txt` allows public pages and blocks `/admin/` and `/api/`.
- `sitemap.xml` includes product pages, case-study pages, service-area pages, blog pages and image URLs.
- Search results are marked `noindex,follow`; the admin area is already `noindex,nofollow`.
- Open Graph and Twitter metadata include page-specific representative images for products, case studies and the gallery.
- JSON-LD now covers the local business, organization, breadcrumbs, product pages, case-study pages, the product index and the gallery.
- Missing blog image references were replaced with existing public assets so indexed pages do not expose broken media.
- Mobile layouts use one-column cards at phone widths, wrapped breadcrumbs and a single-column gallery masonry layout below 640px.
- Public image assets use stable, descriptive URL paths instead of WhatsApp filenames.

## On-page SEO completed

- The gallery exposes only `All`, `Boat & Ship`, `Upholstery`, `Canopy`, `Super Jet Floor Mat Kit` and `Jet Ski Custom Seats` filters.
- Generic gallery images are assigned only to `Boat & Ship`, `Upholstery` or `Canopy`.
- Floor-mat and custom-seat images are isolated in their own categories and product galleries.
- New case studies target the search intent for Super Jet floor mat kits and Jet Ski custom seats in Kuwait.
- Case-study pages contain descriptive titles, challenge, solution, result, project images, internal quote CTAs and semantic headings.
- Product pages link to their related case studies to improve crawl paths and topical relevance.
- Image alt text describes the vessel, work type and view without keyword stuffing.
- Service records were restored for the existing Boat & Ship Seats and Marine Upholstery URLs so internal links resolve correctly.

## Off-page actions for the business owner

- Claim and fully complete the Google Business Profile for Shakeel Marine using the exact business name, address, phone number and opening hours shown on the website.
- Add the website, service categories, Shuwaikh Industrial location, current project photos and a quote link to the profile.
- Keep the same NAP details on Kuwait business directories, marine directories, map listings and social profiles.
- Request genuine customer reviews after completed work; ask customers to mention the actual service and Kuwait location naturally.
- Publish the new project photos on active Instagram, Facebook and TikTok profiles with links back to the relevant product or case-study URL.
- Build relevant local links through marinas, boating clubs, water-sports communities, suppliers and Kuwait business publications.
- Do not buy bulk links, publish copied articles or create fabricated reviews and project metrics.
- Submit the sitemap in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, then monitor indexing, queries, Core Web Vitals and local-pack visibility monthly.

## Ongoing content plan

- Add one original case study for each substantial completed project with the customer's permission.
- Add vessel model, material, location and project date only when verified by the business.
- Refresh service and area pages with real photographs, FAQs and completed-work references rather than repeated doorway copy.

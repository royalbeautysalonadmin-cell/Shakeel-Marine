- Format: MP4 (H.264)
- Resolution: 1920x1080 or higher
- Duration: 10-30 seconds (it loops)
- Size: Under 10MB for fast loading
- Content: Jet ski, boat, marine upholstery, ocean, canopy — any premium marine footage
